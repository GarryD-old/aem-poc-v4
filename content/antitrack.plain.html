FAQs

Why would I need anti-tracking software?

Anti-tracking software helps protect your online privacy by preventing advertisers, data brokers, and websites from collecting information about your browsing habits and using it to build a profile about you.

What is anti-tracking?

Anti-tracking refers to tools and techniques that prevent companies from monitoring your online activity. This includes blocking cookies, masking your digital fingerprint, and stopping trackers from following you across websites.

How can a tracker blocker protect my online privacy?

A tracker blocker detects and stops tracking scripts from loading on websites you visit. It prevents companies from collecting your browsing data, creating profiles, and targeting you with personalized ads or variable pricing.

Who could be tracking me online?

Many entities can track you online including advertisers, social media platforms, data brokers, your internet service provider, and even the websites you visit directly. They use cookies, fingerprinting, and other techniques to follow your activity.

Is AVG AntiTrack the same as a VPN?

No. A VPN hides your IP address and encrypts your connection, while AVG AntiTrack specifically blocks fingerprinting and tracking technologies. For the most comprehensive privacy protection, we recommend using both together.
