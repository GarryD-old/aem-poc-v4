FAQs

Why does your Mac need antivirus protection?

Macs are not immune to malware. As Macs have grown in popularity, so have the threats targeting them — including viruses, ransomware, spyware, and phishing scams. Antivirus protection helps keep your Mac and personal data safe from these evolving threats.

Why choose AVG Internet Security for Mac?

AVG Internet Security offers award-winning antivirus, advanced ransomware protection, network security, and protection against fake and phishing websites. Plus you can try it free before you buy.

Can I use AVG Internet Security on multiple devices simultaneously?

Yes. With a multi-device subscription you can protect up to 10 devices at the same time. A single-device subscription covers one device.

Can I use AVG Internet Security on multiple platforms?

The multi-device subscription works across macOS, Windows, and Android. A single-device Mac subscription is for Mac only.

Do I need to uninstall AVG AntiVirus FREE before upgrading to AVG Internet Security?

No. The upgrade happens automatically once you activate your Internet Security subscription. You can also activate it manually if needed.

How do I do a security check on my Mac?

AVG Internet Security automatically scans your Mac for threats and removes them. You can also run a manual scan at any time from the app.

How can I transfer my AVG Internet Security subscription to another device?

Uninstall AVG from the old device, install it on the new device, and reactivate using the AVG Account linked to your subscription.
